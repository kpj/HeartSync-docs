Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

1. Overview — 100% On-Device Processing

HeartSync does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, health data, or usage analytics on external servers. All data accessed or generated by HeartSync remains exclusively on your iPhone and within your personal Apple Health database.

2. Apple HealthKit Integration

With your explicit permission, HeartSync integrates with Apple HealthKit to provide accurate heart rate zones, calorie expenditure, and workout recording:

  • Health Data Read: Biological Sex, Date of Birth (to estimate maximum heart rate via the Tanaka formula), Body Mass (Weight), and Resting Heart Rate. These values are read locally in memory solely to compute your personalized 5 heart rate zones and active/resting calorie burn during workouts.
  • Health Data Written: Completed Workout Sessions (HKWorkout), continuous 1 Hz Heart Rate samples, Active Energy Burned, Basal (Resting) Energy Burned, and Workout Effort Score (Training Load).

In full compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Section 2.5.1 and 5.1.1):

  • HeartSync never transfers HealthKit information to any external server, cloud service, or third party.
  • HeartSync never uses HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, or use-based data mining.

3. Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Access

HeartSync uses Bluetooth solely to discover, connect to, and stream real-time heart rate and battery level measurements from standard Bluetooth SIG Heart Rate Service (0x180D) sensors. The identifier of your last connected sensor is stored locally in UserDefaults on your device to enable automatic reconnection and is deleted whenever you tap Forget Device or uninstall the app.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

HeartSync contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics trackers, no crash-reporting services, and no advertising frameworks.
