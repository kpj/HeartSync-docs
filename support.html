Support & FAQ

Need help pairing your sensor or syncing workouts with Apple Health?

How do I connect my Bluetooth heart rate monitor?

  1. Turn on your Bluetooth heart rate sensor (e.g., Polar Verity Sense, Polar H10, Wahoo TICKR, Garmin HRM) and ensure it is in heart rate broadcast mode.
  2. Do not pair it in iOS Settings → Bluetooth (if it is already paired in iOS Settings or connected to another app, disconnect/forget it there so it can broadcast freely).
  3. Open HeartSync, tap the sensor status pill in the top-right corner, and select your sensor under Discovered Devices.

Why aren't my workouts showing in Apple Health or Fitness Rings?

Ensure HeartSync has permission to read and write Health data:

Open iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → HeartSync and tap Turn On All.

Does HeartSync continue tracking while my iPhone screen is locked?

Yes! HeartSync uses Apple's native CoreBluetooth background mode (bluetooth-central) to receive continuous 1 Hz heart rate updates from your sensor and calculate active calories even while your iPhone screen is locked on the sidelines.
